# SABSA

SABSA provides a business-driven security architecture method that
traces business requirements and attributes into security architecture.

ORSM complements this by making **operational resilience** an explicit
architectural concern.

A useful distinction is:

-   **SABSA:** what should be designed from business requirements?
-   **ORSM:** how effectively does that design continue to support the
    organisation once it is operating?

ORSM measures such as complexity, recoverability, human friction and
dependency resilience can provide an operational layer alongside SABSA's
business attribute traceability.
