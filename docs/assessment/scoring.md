# Scoring

ORSM domains may be scored on a five-point scale.

    Score Meaning
  ------- ------------
    **1** Poor
    **2** Developing
    **3** Acceptable
    **4** Good
    **5** Excellent

A seven-domain assessment therefore has a nominal maximum score of
**35**.

## Important interpretation

!!! warning "A score is an indicator, not the assurance outcome" A high
aggregate score must not conceal a serious weakness in an individual
domain. ORSM is intended to support architectural judgement, not become
a compliance percentage.

The framework should therefore record the score alongside:

-   supporting evidence;
-   material risks;
-   recommendations;
-   trend over time; and
-   the resulting architectural decision.

## Example

  Domain                               Score
  ---------------------------- -------------
  Protection Intent                        4
  Complexity Management                    3
  Operational Sustainability               4
  Operational Resilience                   5
  Recovery Engineering                     4
  Human Factors                            3
  Dependency Resilience                    4
  **Total**                      **27 / 35**

The source framework describes 27/35 as 77% and an operational rating of
**Good**.

For ORSM 2.1, the preferred direction is to ensure that the detailed
domain findings remain more important than the aggregate percentage.
